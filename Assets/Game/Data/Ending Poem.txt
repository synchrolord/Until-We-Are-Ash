we left our names in the ash,
so the smoke would remember us.

a furnace full of flowers
opened where no sun could reach.

i thought the fire would save us.
it only taught the roots our warmth.

when i ask for water, give me fire.
when i stop screaming, burn what answers.

no one leaves until the roots are ash.
nothing leaves but smoke.

you were the last thing i saw burning.
i will burn so nothing follows.

come burn with me
in the cinders of my heart—

UNTIL WE ARE ASH
